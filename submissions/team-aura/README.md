# Project title  :  AURA

## Project Overview

**AURA** is an initiative focused on achieving **Zero Defect by AI Agents** through intelligent automation across the product development life cycle.

By using AI-driven workflows, AURA aims to reduce manual effort, improve consistency, detect issues early, and increase delivery quality throughout the engineering process.

## Problem Statement

**Zero defect Product Development**

Modern product development involves repetitive checks, manual handoffs, and delayed defect detection. These gaps lead to rework, quality issues, and slower releases. The challenge is to use AI agents effectively to minimize defects and improve development efficiency.

## Solution Summary

**Product development life cycle automation using AURA**

AURA automates key activities across the product development life cycle using AI agents. It supports teams by streamlining processes such as validation, monitoring, quality checks, and workflow orchestration. The goal is to enable faster, more reliable, and higher-quality software delivery with minimal defects.

## Key Objectives

- Reduce defects through AI-assisted validation and automation
- Improve development speed and operational efficiency
- Automate repetitive tasks across the SDLC
- Enhance quality and consistency in delivery
- Support zero-defect engineering practices
